## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, advised by [Prof. Ce Yang](https://bbe.umn.edu/people/ce-yang). I study how to make foundation models **reliable in expert domains**, where labels are scarce, mistakes are costly, and general-purpose models often lack the knowledge to be trusted. My research builds AI systems that ground their decisions in **expert knowledge** and **verifiable evidence**, with agriculture as my flagship domain.

My work follows three threads. **Knowledge-grounded learning:** using structured expert knowledge in place of large-scale annotation, such as insect life stages for few-shot recognition ([Pest-MoE]({{ '/projects/pest-moe.html' | relative_url }})) and expert-curated benchmarks that test whether LLM advice can be trusted ([IPM-Bench]({{ '/projects/ipm-bench.html' | relative_url }})). **Verification and evaluation:** teaching multimodal models to check their predictions against external evidence ([GeoArbiter](https://dbcopper.github.io/projects/geoarbiter.html)) and to score candidate answers efficiently ([AgriJev](https://dbcopper.github.io/projects/agrijev.html)). **Perception and decision-making under uncertainty:** reconstructing crops in 3D from repeated UAV surveys ([UAV3DCrop](https://dbcopper.github.io/projects/uav3dcrop.html)), mapping crops from satellite image time series ([PhenoStitch](https://dbcopper.github.io/projects/phenostitch.html)), deciding where to look and when to stop collecting evidence ([Count-to-Confidence](https://dbcopper.github.io/projects/count-to-confidence.html)), and building agents that turn these models into practical phenotyping workflows ([PhenoAgent](https://phenoagent-showcase.pages.dev/)).

I previously interned at Ant Group, where I trained reward models for text-to-image generation from human preferences. I hold a master’s degree in Electronic Information and a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering.
